# Open questions & risks

Things we don't know yet. Move an item to [[decisions]] (or delete it) once resolved.

- **Can a 60 kg humanoid learn to get up?** Prior now strong YES: the ~70 kg H1-2 did it (arXiv:2603.08619, sim 93.4%, hardware 10/10) with WORSE specific leg torque than the A3 (knee 2.9 vs our 3.1 Nm/kg·m). Waist-pitch worry RESOLVED: the proven route is a HIP sit-up (HoST's G1 has no waist pitch at all; our hip range beats theirs). Remaining unknown is only motion QUALITY at 60 kg (HoST's H1-2 community port "stood but behaved strangely"). Knee range 145° vs G1's 165° is our one kinematic deficit — watch for hip compensation in eval videos.
- **PD gains, armature, and default pose are assumptions**, not AgiBot specs (marked in the manifest). Must be replaced with real actuator specs (AgiBot docs or A3 SDK) before any sim-to-real claim. Single edit point: `configs/robots/a3_ultra.yaml`.
- **Get-up reward weights are first-guess, untuned.** The extension's staged weights (upright 1 / height 2 / target-pose 2.5 / stand-still 1.5) and assist-curriculum thresholds are HoST-inspired but never trained to convergence. Expect iteration on the first Lambda runs; watch `getup_success_rate` vs `getup_assist_scale` coupling (assist must reach 0 or the policy is a lie). **The v3 curriculum deadlock is fixed ([[decisions]] 2026-08-18), but the fix is unvalidated by training** — it is verified only against a standing spawn in MuJoCo. What the next run must actually demonstrate: `getup_action_authority` falls on schedule, `getup_rose_rate_fallen` rises off the floor, and `getup_success_rate` leaves 0 once β reaches 1.0. Abort rules are in `scripts/cloud/train_a3_getup_cloud.sh`.
- **Does the rollover leg pass? (still open, 2026-08-18)** `train_a3_getup_cloud.sh` runs `a3-ultra-rollover` before `a3-ultra-getup`, and its result was never checked. Gate: `getup_success_rate > 0.95` on the settled-on-back criterion. Rollover is unaffected by the β deadlock (it overrides `getup_easy_start_prob` to 0, fixes authority at 1.0, disables the assist), so it may well have passed — which would make supine get-up the only open half of M2. **No rollover checkpoint was ever pulled off the instance**, so this cannot be answered locally; read `Env/getup_success_rate` off that run's TensorBoard, or scp the checkpoint and run `scripts/eval/getup_video.py --task rollover` (added 2026-08-18 — the get-up gate scores a working rollover policy 0/N, since the two criteria are near-opposites).
- **`SUCCESS_JOINT_VEL = 1.0 rad/s` is a deploy contract being used as a training signal.** The manifest's handoff tolerance is right for a *deterministic* deployed policy (the locomotion policy sits 50× inside it), but a stochastic policy sampling actions at 50 Hz spikes past it constantly. The leaky counter + EMA make it usable; whether 1.0 is the right number at all — versus a percentile, or a mean over legs+waist — is still unexamined. The eval harness keeps the strict instantaneous contract, so the two numbers are not identically defined.
- ~~**Assist-force IsaacSim branch never executed**~~ — **executed, 2026-08-14 (E13) and 2026-08-16 (E13b).** Both cloud runs applied the wrench for their full length; E13's assisted success 0.30 against E13-eval's unassisted 0/12 is direct evidence it changes behaviour. `set_external_force_and_torque` is deprecated in IsaacLab 2.3 → DeprecationWarning spam is expected and harmless. Still worth watching on the next run: base_velocity termination spikes in the first ~50 iterations (bank poses were settled in MuJoCo, PhysX geometry differs — mitigated by the +1 cm spawn clearance).
- **Holosoma MuJoCo-backend dof-order bug (found in get-up review, affects locomotion smoke too).** The MuJoCo/MJWarp wrapper orders dof arrays in MJCF TREE order (A3: waist-first) but fills `dof_vel_limits`/`torque_limits`/`dof_pos_limits` POSITIONALLY from the config lists (canonical legs-first) → scrambled per-joint limits on that backend only (G1 unaffected: its tree order == config order; IsaacSim unaffected: resolves by name with preserve_order). Local consequences: action-term torque clipping and `limits_dof_pos` reward use scrambled limits in ALL local MJWarp smoke runs (incl. locomotion E06 variant). The get-up extension now builds its own name-mapped limit tensors and is immune on every backend. Consider reporting upstream to amazon-far/holosoma. Cloud IsaacSim runs are unaffected.
- **Early reward is penalty-dominated until first rises.** At the penalty floor (scale 0.1) an untrained policy pays ~0.074/step action-rate against ~0.01–0.05/step task reward; the assist force creating height reward is what breaks the "lie still" optimum. Watch `rew_penalty_action_rate` vs task terms in the first 500 iterations; if the policy goes limp, drop PenaltyCurriculum `initial_scale`/`min_scale` to 0.05.
- **v2 ladder run once; S0/S1 passed, S2–S4 must be re-run (2026-08-16).** See [[experiments]] E09. S0 and S1 both clear **68/68** on the stability grid and are real candidates; S2–S4 were invalidated by the action-rate masking bug and the cold-start iteration budget (both fixed, [[decisions]]). The re-run is S1 (retrain at the unified 692-dim contract) → S2 → S3 → S4, chained by checkpoint, ~15 GPU-h. Still open: (a) whether S1 alone is enough to ship — it already fixes the two defects visible in the E08 videos, and everything after it is arm/terrain capability rather than walking quality; (b) whether to take 100 Hz control (`--simulator.config.sim.control-decimation 2`), which roughly doubles wall clock and changes the ONNX control period (the layout metadata carries it, so the harness follows automatically).
- ~~**`rew_tracking_ang_vel` still misses its own 0.8 gate**~~ — **resolved 2026-08-18: the gate was measuring the wrong thing and is replaced.** The defect it stood for is uncommanded yaw *drift*, which that scalar does not measure — it is an `exp()` of a squared error sitting at **92% of its maximum** at S1's error, so it saturates long before the error is small, which is exactly how it moved the wrong way (0.559 → 0.538) while the real angular error more than halved. Drift is now measured directly: `yaw_drift_dps` is printed per scenario, summarised per group as `max_abs_yaw_drift_dps`, and logged in training as `Env/yaw_drift_dps` / `Env/heading_err_deg`. The promotion gates are in [[decisions]] 2026-08-18 (T1), stated in deg/s.
- ~~**The CAM reward's magnitude is an assumption**~~ — **resolved 2026-08-16 by measurement.** The first S2/S3/S4 runs logged `Env/cam_z` at **0.0120–0.0136** m²/s against the assumed `CAM_REF_AMPLITUDE = 0.01`. The limb-momentum estimate was right to within 25%, so the constant stays as it is. (The `Env/cam_xy` companion reads 0.032–0.036, i.e. horizontal CAM runs ~3× the vertical, as expected for a walker.) Both CAM terms use the same constant; nothing further to tune.
- **Arm-amplitude curriculum ran away, not stalled (2026-08-16).** The predicted failure was the opposite of what happened. With `level_up_threshold = 750` against a 1001-step cap, the first S2 run widened to the 100% maximum within 20k iterations and stayed pinned there while the policy was *losing ground* — 800-step episodes cleared the gate on every reset even though a fifth of all episodes were ending in a fall. **A gate that a failing policy passes is not a gate.** Thresholds are now 950 up / 800 down, near the cap. Still open: whether episode length is the right signal at all, or whether the curriculum should key off `rew_tracking_lin_vel` (`docs/final_rl_policy.md` §8) — re-check `Env/arm_amplitude` against `Env/net_reward_per_step` on the next S2.
- ~~**Upper-body skills need an interface decision**~~ — **decided 2026-08-18** ([[decisions]]). Retraining with driven arms was tried and **made robustness worse in every observation treatment** (S4 never clears 16/28 against v1's 28/28, [[experiments]] E09c). Arm skills become a **physics-level disturbance**; the policy keeps all 29 action channels and is never action-overridden. Masking stays the interim deploy contract for S1 only (28/28) — and it must mask **both** arm channels, since hiding the target alone is worse than hiding nothing.
- **How is the arm disturbance actually injected? (new, 2026-08-18).** The decision is made; the mechanism is not. Options: a PD target offset applied on top of the policy's arm target, an external torque on the arm links, or a wrench on the hands. It has to (a) leave the policy's action reaching the simulator so no channel loses its gradient, (b) reproduce in MuJoCo for the sim2sim gate, and (c) span the *eval* distribution — 3 Hz swings and 0.5 s slams between joint extremes, not `UpperBodyCommand`'s 0.2–2.0 Hz. Getting (c) wrong is what made component C worse than doing nothing.
- **Does `upper_body_target` stay in the observation? (new, 2026-08-18).** It was added so the policy could *anticipate* a skill's next pose. With the skill as a disturbance the policy may not need or want it, and S1's ablation is a warning: hiding that channel alone drops it to 8/28, below hiding nothing, because displaced arms plus a zeroed target is an incoherent pair. Either keep it always-live or remove it — do not ship a policy whose deploy contract requires zeroing it selectively.
- **Removing the ownership machinery is cleanup, not a behaviour change (new, 2026-08-18).** `penalty_arm_off_target` and the `_policy_owned_mask` on `pose` / `penalty_action_rate` / `penalty_action_jerk` / `penalty_dof_acc` / `penalty_torques` exist only because channels were being discarded. With no override, `_policy_owned_mask` returns `None` and all of them are exact no-ops (pinned in `tests/test_v2_reward_masking.py`). Delete them when the disturbance path lands rather than carrying dead machinery.
- **Does freeing the yaw chain cost anything we care about? (new, 2026-08-18).** T1 drops `hip_yaw` 5.0 → 1.0 and `waist_yaw` 50.0 → 1.0 in `pose`. The mechanism is well evidenced ([[experiments]] E09d) but the side effects are not measured: a policy free to twist its waist may walk with a visible torso yaw oscillation even while tracking perfectly, and `waist_yaw` also carries the arms, so the arm-suite masked contract (28/28) should be re-run and not assumed. Watch `Env/net_reward_per_step`, action jitter, and `scripts/eval/sim2sim_arms.py --arm-obs none` on the first T1 checkpoint.
- **Is the +1.124/step of new positive reward safe? (new, 2026-08-18).** The three tracking-precision terms add ~7x S1's measured net per-step reward, diluting the penalty terms' relative pull — the policy is being invited to trade smoothness for precision. Intended, but S4 regressed on exactly this axis. The local MJWarp smoke could not confirm it (TensorBoard scalars do not emit on that path, and the run hits the documented `nefc overflow` → NaN at ~iteration 28), so **`Env/net_reward_per_step` and `Env/average_episode_length` must be read off the first cloud logging point** before letting T1 run to 90k. Abort if net goes negative or episode length falls off the 1000 cap.
- **Which single ingredient breaks the alpine combo?** Rough d1.0, 15° climb, 15° descent, µ0.2 and 2 m/s shoves are each survivable alone; combining them is not (falls at 2.3–8.3 s). Worth an ablation before the Everest fine-tune, so M4 targets the actual coupling rather than adding difficulty blindly.
- **Does the v5 asset change locomotion behavior?** v5 adds 16 collision boxes over the E01 asset and makes hip links contact-capable — "hip" termination fires slightly earlier on falls. Watch nefc counters on the first v5 locomotion run.
- **Terrain handoff not delivered.** Tomasz's Everest terrain generator plugs into `TerrainSpec`/`TerrainPatch`; E10/E15 (the whole Everest fine-tune phase) blocked until it lands. Interim option: GeologicDome's existing reconstructions.
- ~~**ONNX action scaling**~~ — resolved 2026-08-14. The export embeds both the observation normalizer and the per-joint action bounds, so the consumer applies only the uniform `action_scale` 0.25 around the default pose. `docs/onnx_policy_interface.md` was also wrong about the observation order (it is alphabetical by term name) and the phase width (2, not 1); the verified contract now lives in `everest_locomotion/evaluation/sim2sim.py`.
- **W&B credentials not configured** — TensorBoard only for now.
- **FastSAC vs PPO on A3** (E07) — undecided until both are reasonably tuned.
